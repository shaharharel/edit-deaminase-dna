### WHAT THIS SETTLES
THE +0.075 "CLONE-LUCK FLOOR" QUOTED ALL SESSION WAS NOT A GENERAL ESTIMATE. It was one
variant's value, and it is the SMALLER of the only two ever measured. Same-editor variability

on qualified clones against the same estimator.

### WHAT IT DOES NOT SETTLE
Which floor is right. Against 0.075 the Y130G mean of +0.357 is 4.8 sigma; against 0.237 it is
1.5 sigma. WITH TWO ESTIMATES OF A SPREAD YOU CANNOT CHOOSE BETWEEN THEM. That is the same
lesson as everywhere else today -- n=2 does not estimate a spread, it only demonstrates one
exists -- and it is now the binding constraint on the entire editor track, from two independent
directions: the clonal-power check said 25-50 clones per arm, and this says the same thing in
the language of variance.

and audit in the standing checklist. AND its margin over same-editor variability is somewhere
between 1.5 and 4.8 sigma, with no way to narrow that from the data in hand. I am not going to
quote the favourable end.
