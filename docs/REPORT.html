Executive summary

We predict guide-independent base-editor DNA off-target locations as a clinical safety prior. After thorough auditing (4 corrected over-claims), the defensible deliverable is an f × g factorized site-level model: f = deaminase motif (a known editor property, provided per editor), g = cross-editor accessibility (learned, transfers). It recovers ~30–37% of held-out off-targets in the top 10% of screened sites (3–4× enrichment), generalizing across treatments and to unseen deaminases — provided their motif spectrum is given as input.

What works validated

  • Motif (f) is deaminase-specific — provide per editor
  • Accessibility (g) is deaminase-agnostic — transfers
  • f × g generalizes across treatments AND novel deaminases (with motif input)
  • Site-level (motif-aware) beats bin-level (motif-blind) by ~10 pp recall
  • Cell-type-robust at 1 Mb resolution

What doesn't refuted

  • Zero-shot to a novel deaminase without motif input (Yu: 1.2×)
  • Within-bin / per-site exact-C prediction beyond motif+accessibility (stochastic)
  • Cancer-driver enrichment as biology (mostly gene-density)
  • Per-site / per-gene calibrated absolute rate (no cross-assay gold)
  • Deeper architectures helping (data, not arch, is the ceiling)

Methods

Features

Per 1 Mb bin (2,838 bins)

eligible_C
Count of opportunity bases (C+G on the genome strand) in the bin
tpc_count
Count of TpC (and reverse-comp GpA) motifs — sequence opportunity
gene_density
Distinct protein-coding genes centered in the bin (0–64; refGene) — opportunity / ascertainment proxy. The dominant confound (r=0.61 with edit count); controlled in every claim.
accessibility (4 tracks)
DNase-seq (open chromatin), ATAC-seq (open chromatin), R-loop / MapR (ssDNA exposure), H3K27ac (active enhancers/promoters) — mean ENCODE signal in the bin. Captures where ssDNA is exposed for the deaminase.
mappability
k=100 Umap multi-track mappability — call-bias control
conservation
phyloP / phastCons 100-way (used in earlier ablations)

Per site (candidate C or A)

local motif (±10 one-hot)
80-dim flattened one-hot of the ±10 flanking sequence (center base dropped — it is the edited C/A by construction)
local motif (±25 + CNN)
4 × 51 one-hot for the CNN architecture
regional accessibility (4)
The site's 1 Mb bin DNase/ATAC/R-loop/H3K27ac
deaminase identity (3)
One-hot of family {rAPOBEC1, engineered, TadA} OR the deaminase's trinucleotide motif spectrum

Models

  • Trinuc lookup — 16-feature LR; the f-baseline (saturates the motif signal)
  • ±10 one-hot + MLP — local motif via simple MLP (64-h, dropout 0.3)
  • ±25 one-hot + 1D CNN — Conv(4→32,k=7)+Conv(32→32,k=5)+AdaptiveMaxPool
  • Deep MLP — 128→64→32 with dropout 0.3
  • HyenaDNA — long-context FM, mean-pool and center-token variants (tested in sister project; pinned transformers 4.39.3)
  • Bin-level MLP (g) — 32-h on aggregated bin features; LOCO target = log1p(edit count)
  • f × g deployment — score = z(motif log-odds spectrum) + z(g-network on accessibility); g trained on OTHER sources (LOSO), f estimated from a disjoint chromosome partition (non-circular)

Evaluation

  • LOCO (leave-one-chromosome-out) — Spearman
  • LOSO (leave-one-source-out) — cross-source generalization
  • Held-out chromosome split — site-level (odd / even)
  • Held-out deaminase / treatment — full source held out
  • Background ladder — random base-matched · trinucleotide-matched · bin-matched · mappability-matched
  • Recall @ top-K% and enrichment = recall/K (portable across pos:bg ratios)
  • AUROC, Spearman, Brier (where applicable)
  • Within-stratum control (gene-density quintile) — for any "chromatin adds beyond" claim
  • Circular-shift null — autocorrelation-controlled increment

Cross-deaminase DNA Editing Index — recovering signal from bulk clonal WGS NEW

Motivation

Two limits documented elsewhere in this report bound the location model: the Doman BE4 clonal WGS was rejected (per-site germline-dominated; G3/G4), and bulk-population guide-independent WGS is per-site undetectable (off-target VAF <1% sits below 30–50× WGS sensitivity). The location model also has no per-gene "how much" layer (it ranks where, not magnitude; "per-gene calibrated absolute rate" is listed as refuted). This experiment reframes the question: don't call any single site — pool millions of coding positions into a per-cohort, per-gene ratio index (Levanon-style editing index). The aggregate signal is recoverable even when no individual site is, turning the "rejected" WGS into usable cross-deaminase ground truth and supplying the magnitude layer.

Experiment design (short)

  • Data: 12 Doman clones — BE4 ×6, YE1-BE4 ×3, nCas9 ×2 (deaminase-free control), Parent ×1 — hg19, over a fixed panel of 15,666,970 coding C/G positions.
  • Germline whitelist: drop positions where the untreated Parent shows >5% variant reads. Light: 98.2% of positions kept (15,379,625).
  • Index: pool reads across a cohort's clones; per gene compute the C→T edited fraction (a ratio → coverage-invariant) for TpC (APOBEC1 motif) vs non-TpC (negative control; CpG already excluded). DEI = treated − Parent. Motif-corrected = DEITpC − DEInon-TpC.
  • Validation: split-half reproducibility within BE4 (clones {1,2,3} vs {5,6,7}); coverage & burden-concentration controls; per-site recovery of published BE4 off-targets.

The discriminator works. Raw burden is misleading — the deaminase-free nCas9 control sits at 1.57× Parent (higher than the real safer editor), because each clone carries its own somatic background. But only BE4 has a positive motif-corrected DEI: TpC-specific editing above both the untreated baseline and the gene's general mutability. The motif-corrected index sees through the clonal-somatic confound that fools raw C→T.
  • Reproducible: split-half BE4 (3 v 3) per-gene DEI Spearman 0.452 (motif-corrected 0.545); top-500 hotspot overlap 4.3× chance. Beats the earlier 2-clone POC (0.41) → more clones lift reproducibility.
  • Not a coverage artifact: DEI(ratio) vs gene size Spearman −0.028 (≈0); raw edit-count vs size +0.797 (the trap the ratio avoids).
  • Concentrated: 50% of BE4 excess edits fall in the top 14.7% of genes.

Main outcomes & honest limits

What works validated

  • Aggregate editing index is recoverable from bulk clonal WGS by pooling — sidesteps the per-site VAF ceiling
  • Motif-corrected DEI is the only cohort-level discriminator that defeats the nCas9 clonal-somatic confound
  • Editor-class safety ranking (BE4 dirty ≫ YE1 safe ≈ no-editor) and top-hotspot ranking are reproducible

What's weak / refuted honest

  • Per-gene resolution: matched BE4 vs nCas9 motif-corrected DEI separates only 50.5% of genes (Wilcoxon p=2.9e-3) — signal lives in a hotspot tail
  • Per-site recovery of published BE4 off-targets is null: of 27,529 catalog sites only 155 fall in the coding panel, 40 testable, 0 editing — consistent with the sub-VAF ceiling (individual sites stay undetectable; only the pooled cohort signal is robust)
  • Top-hotspot claim is currently internal reproducibility, not yet externally/clinically anchored

Next steps — toward prediction

  1. Finish 24 clones (BE4 8/8 ✓; YE1-BE4 8, nCas9 7, Parent 1 — acquisition in progress) → tighten per-gene power.
  2. Stage-2 Beta-Binomial shrinkage → calibrated per-gene confidence intervals; directly attacks the "per-gene calibrated absolute rate" gap (target Brier / ECE < 0.05).
  3. Couple to the f × g model: use per-gene DEI as the ground-truth magnitude label to (a) calibrate/validate the location prior and (b) train a per-gene risk head. The retained per-site label table (15.4 M sites, coordinates kept) feeds Evo sequence-context windows.
  4. Clinical anchor (open): coding off-target catalogs barely overlap our CDS panel (0.6%), so anchor instead via cancer/essential-gene-category enrichment (COSMIC Tier-1 / ClinGen HI / DepMap) once per-gene power suffices, and the canonical APOB Q2153X substrate.

f / g decomposition — the conceptual frame

Guide-independent off-target activity factorizes cleanly into a deaminase preference (f) that is editor-specific and acts at single-base resolution, and a regional ssDNA-exposure landscape (g) that is approximately editor-agnostic and operates at ≥1 Mb resolution. The two have very different ML characters and very different ceilings.

f (deaminase preference)

validated A trinucleotide motif. No ML headroom.

  • Trinuc-only LR: AUROC 0.826 on cross-cell-type transfer (HEK293 → iPSC)
  • ±10 one-hot MLP: 0.82 — no improvement over trinuc
  • HyenaDNA mean-pool: 0.56 · HyenaDNA center-token: 0.64 — both lose to one-hot
  • Per-editor cosine similarity (HEK vs iPSC rAPOBEC1): 0.996
  • Engineered/dual deaminases differ → per-editor motif lookup required (a known biochemical property)

g (≥1 Mb landscape)

validated Real, modest, learnable; data-bound.

  • LOCO Spearman ~0.63 — but dominated by gene density (r=0.61 with edit count)
  • Chromatin delta over a baseline that already includes GC + gene-density + motif + mappability: +0.04 (down from a naive +0.09 when gene density isn't controlled — the confound this project corrected)
  • Within-gene-density-quintile (control-by-design): chromatin Spearman 0.351 (DNase alone 0.25)
  • LOSO Spearman: 0.37–0.56 — the honest cross-source number
  • Circular-shift null: ~0 (autocorrelation-controlled)
  • Cell-type-robust: DNase HEK / iPSC / HSPC corr 0.995 at 1 Mb
  • Bin-level deep nets / Enformer-style models did not help (N=2,838 is the ceiling)

Generalization between treatments (deaminase-conditioned)

Key conceptual point: the motif is deaminase-specific, not treatment-specific. A "treatment" = deaminase + Cas + guide + dose; only the deaminase determines f. So a model conditioned on the deaminase should generalize from one treatment to another that reuses the same deaminase.

f × g deployment model — the capstone

Make the factorization explicit: provide the editor's known motif spectrum (f) as an input feature; learn accessibility (g) on other sources (LOSO). f estimated from a chromosome partition disjoint from the eval partition (non-circular). Reconciling the numbers — the ladder

The different headline numbers across the project are not contradictions — they are different points on a ladder of difficulty (resolution × background × what's held out). The 24% sits at the hard end of all three axes; 30–37% applies your two key levers (site resolution + deaminase motif); 65–97% drops the hard background.

Honest deliverable

A site-resolution screening prior over candidate C (CBE) / A (ABE) positions, parameterized as f(known deaminase motif spectrum) × g(cross-editor accessibility from DNase / ATAC / R-loop / H3K27ac).

Intended use

  • Triage candidate sites for targeted deep sequencing in BE off-target characterization
  • Rank megabase regions for an editor whose deaminase motif is known (a standard biochemical property)
  • Generalize the regional accessibility component across editors (incl. unseen deaminases)

Out of intended use

  • Cannot clear a region or site (release-spec safety claim requires calibration we don't have)
  • Not calibrated absolute rate; output is a relative rank / enrichment
  • Not target-cell-type specific at 1 Mb (accessibility is cell-type-invariant at that resolution)
  • Not a per-gene cancer-driver flagger (gene-density is the dominant confound)
  • Zero-shot to a brand-new deaminase is degraded — supply its motif

Limits, data ceiling, next steps

The fundamental ceiling

  • N = 2,838 bins, ~9.7 M candidate C's in CDS; per-source positives 2.7k–16k
  • Inter-assay reproducibility at 1 Mb: ~0.26 Spearman — this caps any cross-assay model
  • Bulk-population guide-independent WGS does not exist (sub-1% VAF is below 30–50× WGS sensitivity)
  • Only enrichment assays (Detect-seq, EndoV-seq, CHANGE-seq-BE) yield site tables; bulk treated−control excess landscape is unavailable
  • Architecture is not the bottleneck (deep ≈ shallow; HyenaDNA < one-hot; Enformer/Borzoi deferred)

Levers that would genuinely move the number

  • Prospective wet-lab validation (Levanon collaboration) — deep-sequence gene-density-matched high- vs low-rank regions for one editor; confound-proof, prospective demonstration
  • More clean bulk-genome-wide assays — EndoV-seq, Selict-seq, Buchumenski-style would raise the 0.26 ceiling
  • Pivot to guide-DEPendent — CHANGE-seq-BE (PRJNA1372720) + EndoV-seq are data-rich; clinically the per-therapy question
  • Persist bin builders into the repo (currently in /tmp) — reproducibility gap flagged by QA
  • NB-GLM calibration for relative-rank → calibrated probability

What this project genuinely contributes

  • A clean f × g factorization for guide-independent BE DNA off-targets with explicit transferability rules
  • A QC ladder (G0–G9) that catches the standard confounds (gene-density, mappability, motif-spectrum, germline)
  • A documented data-quality finding: only bulk genome-wide assays capture a predictable regional landscape
  • An honest, audited evaluation framework (background ladder × held-out × enrichment metric) that other BE off-target work can adopt
